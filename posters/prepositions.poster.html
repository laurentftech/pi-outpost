PREPOSITIONS
OF PLACE

📦

Where is it? Look, learn, and say it!

⬆️

ABOVE

The bird is above the house.

⬇️

BELOW

The shoes are below the shelf.

📦

IN

The toy is in the box.

📦

ON

The book is on the table.

📦

UNDER

The cat is under the chair.

🚪

BEHIND

The ball is behind the door.

👀

IN FRONT OF

The bus is in front of the school.

↔️

NEXT TO

The lamp is next to the bed.

Remember: prepositions tell us where things are!
