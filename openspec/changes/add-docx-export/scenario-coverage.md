# Scenario coverage — add-docx-export

Every `#### Scenario:` declared by this change's delta specs, matched to the assertion that would
fail if the contract broke. 41 scenarios: 31 in `docx-export`, 10 in `file`.

Enumerated with `rg '^#### Scenario:' openspec/changes/add-docx-export/specs/`.

Three scenarios are claims about what Word does, which no test here can settle; they are covered by
a recorded observation of real Word instead, in `word-verification.md`. That file also records what
opening the export in LibreOffice found — three defects, all now fixed, none of which any test we
had could see.

One scenario, `OtherReadersSeeThePicture`, is covered by the mechanism that decides it rather than
by direct observation: the only non-Word reader to hand turned out to *support* the SVG extension,
so it never exercised the raster fallback. The limitation is stated in `word-verification.md`.
